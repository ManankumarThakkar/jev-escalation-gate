A 30.5 point gap. A passage that is on topic but silent on the fact is what a retrieval miss really looks like, and 61 of 200 were called answerable when they were not. The 100% is not the good news it looks like: always answering “unanswerable” also scores 100% there, and 0% on real answers.
Where the judgements landed · 400 items, answerable + hard negatives
133 of 200 hard negatives landed in the uncertain band: the gate mostly declines to commit on the class it is worst at. But 10 were accepted at 0.90 or above. Those are the production risk, not the headline accuracy.
