One thing that did not fit in the post. The 0.10 and 0.90 thresholds were not chosen in advance. I swept the full range over these same 400 items and picked the split by reading the curve, so the routing percentages are an in-sample observation, not a validated production estimate. On fresh traffic I would expect them to move.

Same caution on calibration. Aggregate calibration error came out near a resampled noise floor, which sounds reassuring, but that aggregate is carried by one large high-confidence bucket that is mostly easy positives. The mid-range buckets were the least reliable, and those are precisely the scores the escalation band is drawn around. Good enough to route on here, not good enough to fine-tune a threshold with.
