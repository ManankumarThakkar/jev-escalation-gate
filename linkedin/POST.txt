The same answerability gate scored 100% and 69.5% on the same afternoon.

Same model, same prompt, 200 items per slice. The only thing that changed was how I built the wrong answers.

I was testing a cheap pre-check for RAG: before spending a generation call, ask whether the retrieved passage actually contains what the question needs. The judge was Jev, TypeSafe AI's System One model, which returns a typed score instead of prose.

Two kinds of wrong answer:

Random unrelated passage: 200/200 correct.
On topic but missing the fact: 139/200.

The second is what a retrieval miss really looks like. Nothing hands you a passage about jellyfish when you asked about Warsaw. It hands you the right article, missing the one sentence you needed. On those, 61 of 200 were called answerable when they were not.

The 100% is not the good news it looks like either. A classifier that always says "unanswerable" also scores 100% there, and 0% on real answers.

More useful than the accuracy was where the uncertainty landed.

Of the 200 hard negatives, 133 scored in the middle of the range rather than at either end. The gate mostly declined to commit on the class it is worst at. It confidently rejected 57. It confidently accepted 10, and those 10 are the real production risk.

Which makes the routing less dramatic than it first looks:

At or below 0.10, drop the passage and retrieve again. 14.5% of judgements, and this is the only branch that saves a generation call.

Between 0.10 and 0.90, escalate the judgement. 42.5%.

At or above 0.90, pass it through to generation. That is still an LLM call. The gate decides whether to generate, not what to generate.

Limits. 600 decisions, one English dataset. The thresholds were picked by reading this same data, so those splits are in-sample rather than validated. Aggregate calibration error sat near a resampled noise floor, but the mid-range buckets were the least reliable, and that is exactly where the escalation band sits. SQuAD 2.0 is public, so contamination is possible and I have not ruled it out.
