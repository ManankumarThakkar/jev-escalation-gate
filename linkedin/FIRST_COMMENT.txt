The detail that surprised me most 👇

I expected reading the failures to confirm the headline. It did the opposite.

Of the 10 the gate was most confident about, at least 6 have the answer sitting in the passage. "How many stories does the Bank of America tower have?" against a passage reading "includes 42 floors." The model was right; SQuAD's label was wrong.

Confidence and label quality moved together. Above 0.90 the errors were mostly the benchmark's. Between 0.50 and 0.90 they were real, and they were almost all one thing: a question perturbed against the passage. Swapped names, an inverted claim, a changed number.

Two caveats I would want if I were reading this. The routing percentages are over the 400 answerable and hard-negative items, not all 600; including the easy-negative control pushes the rejection rate from 14.5% to 42.8%, but 199 of those 257 rejections are the control itself. And the 0.10 / 0.90 thresholds were not set in advance, I picked them by reading the curve on this same data.
